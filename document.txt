Artificial intelligence and machine learning are transforming the landscape of modern cybersecurity. 
Threat detection systems now use neural networks to identify malicious patterns in network traffic. 
Data analysis allows security teams to react to incidents in real-time. 
However, hackers also use AI to create more sophisticated phishing attacks and automated malware. 
The future of digital safety depends on the balance between automated defense and human expertise. 
Cloud computing and big data processing require new approaches to information protection. 
Encryption and multi-factor authentication remains the foundation of user security. 
System administrators must monitor logs and audit access to sensitive databases regularly. 
Cyber attacks are becoming more frequent, targeting financial institutions and healthcare providers. 
Protecting personal information is a priority for every modern software developer.
